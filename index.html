This unique CD-i game is based on the classic Frantic Freddie and was released in 2018 as a nostalgic tribute to the retro era; it is an old arcade classic reimagined with the distinctive style of the CD-i.

The CD-i version features six nostalgic levels, each accompanied by cheerful, upbeat music. For enthusiasts of forgotten games, retro hardware, and the unique history of the Philips CD-i, this is a charming piece of gaming nostalgia that evokes a time when games could still be wonderfully simple, colorful, and—above all—surprising.
